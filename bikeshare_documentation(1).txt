The dataset was downloaded from kaggle.com:
https://www.kaggle.com/hmavrodiev/london-bike-sharing-dataset

The dataset was created by Transport for London (https://tfl.gov.uk), a government agency that provides datasets to the public (https://tfl.gov.uk/info-for/open-data-users/).

The data were originally acquired from 3 sources: 

- Https://cycling.data.tfl.gov.uk/ 'Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database rights 2016' and Geomni UK Map data © and database rights [2019] 'Powered by TfL Open Data' 
- freemeteo.com - weather data 
- https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays 

The data from cycling dataset is grouped by "Start time", this represent the count of new bike shares grouped by hour. The long duration shares are not taken in the count.

Includes data from 1/1/2015 to 31/12/2016. Each row represents the number of new bike shares in a particular hour on a particular date.

Columns:

"timestamp" - timestamp field for grouping the data 
"month" - the month
"cnt" - the count of a new bike shares during that hour
"t1" - real temperature in F 
"t2" - temperature in F "feels like" 
"hum" - humidity in percentage 
"wind_speed" - wind speed in km/h 
"weather" - category of the weather conditions
"is_holiday" - boolean field - TRUE if the day is a holiday
"is_weekend" - boolean field - TRUE if the day is weekend 
"season" - category field  representing the meteorological season: spring ; summer; fall; winter.
